Detailed Description

Quantity one trade print removed from phantom liquidity at one price, kept until a level update stamped at or after it accounts for it.

Credits are held per trade timestamp, never summed across timestamps: a level update reflects only the trades that preceded it. See Symbol::creditTradedQty.
