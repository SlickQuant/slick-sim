◆ to_hyperliquid_number()

std::string slick::sim::md_publisher::to_hyperliquid_number ( int_fast64_t value)
inline

Formats a fixed-point price or size as Hyperliquid does on the wire.

The venue uses shortest-round-trip floats with at least one fractional digit: 77052 becomes "77052.0", while 0.73245 and 1.915 keep their existing digits. Use to_fixed_string with a one-digit minimum. In a session capture, all 201 distinct prices ended in ".0" and none of the 8807 distinct sizes had a trailing zero.

This matters for clients that key levels by the received price string. Coinbase uses to_price_string/to_qty_string instead and renders whole values without the fractional digit, such as "99".
