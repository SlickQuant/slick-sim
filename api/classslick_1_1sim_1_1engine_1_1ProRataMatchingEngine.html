Detailed Description

Pro-rata matching: at each price level an aggressor that cannot take the whole level is split across its orders by size, not by queue position.

Per level, for incoming quantity Q against orders of total T:

  • Q >= T fills every order, as FIFO would.
  • Otherwise fifo_percent of Q (rounded down to lots) goes FIFO first, then the rest is split in proportion to each order's remaining size, rounded down to lots, with shares under min_allocation dropped to 0, and whatever is left goes FIFO with no minimum. Each resting order gets one fill per aggressor per level, in queue order.

One instance serves every symbol of a venue and is only ever called from the exchange thread, so its scratch buffer needs no synchronisation.
