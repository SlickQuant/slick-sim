◆ clientIdFor()

int Exchange::clientIdFor ( const char *  user_id)
protected

Resolves an order's user_id to the integer identity self-match prevention compares.

Ids are assigned on first sight and stable for the life of the process; an empty user_id yields -1, which isSelfMatch treats as "no identity" and never matches, so an unauthenticated client is left out of SMP rather than being lumped in with every other one.
