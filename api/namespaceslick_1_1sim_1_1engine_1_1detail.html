enum class slick::sim::engine::detail::Reach : uint8_t
strong

How far into a price level an aggressor reaches.

FIFO trades the queue front to back until its quantity runs out. Pro-rata allocates across the whole level, so every order on a level it reaches may trade.

One fill of a client order against a resting order.

remaining is the aggressor's quantity after this fill, so reaching 0 closes the book's batch. full_book_order is the resting order's Order, or nullptr for phantom liquidity. Returns false when the book declines the execution: the aggressor has already been filled and reported by then, so the caller stops rather than unwinding.

One fill of market-data liquidity against a resting order.

There is no aggressor Order to fill or report. qty is the aggressor's remaining quantity: it is decremented by the fill, and restored when the book declines it, in which case this returns false and the caller stops.
